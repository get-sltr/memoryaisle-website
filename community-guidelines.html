Community Guidelines.

Last updated: April 14, 2026 · Effective: April 14, 2026

Community features are not active in the current version of Nourished by Mira. These guidelines govern them if and when they launch, and are published here so the rules are known in advance.

1. Purpose

The Nourished by Mira community exists to provide peer support, share experiences, and exchange practical tips related to nutrition, meal planning, and wellness. It is NOT a forum for medical advice.

2. Rules

What is welcome

  • Sharing recipes and meal ideas
  • Discussing nausea management tips (food-related, not medication-related)
  • Sharing protein-dense meal strategies
  • Discussing grocery shopping experiences
  • Celebrating milestones and progress
  • Sharing workout and training nutrition strategies
  • Asking questions about App features
  • Supporting other community members

What is NOT allowed

  • Medical advice of any kind, including recommendations about medications, dosages, injection sites, or drug interactions
  • Recommending users start, stop, or change any medication
  • Sharing specific compounded medication sources or providers
  • Promoting or selling any product, service, supplement, or medication
  • Sharing other users' personal health information
  • Promoting extreme dietary restriction, disordered eating, or unhealthy behaviors
  • Harassment, bullying, discrimination, or hate speech
  • Misinformation about GLP-1 medications, nutrition science, or health
  • Spam, self-promotion, or commercial solicitation
  • Sharing personal contact information

3. Moderation

All community content is subject to moderation. We use a combination of AI-assisted flagging and human review. Posts that violate community guidelines will be removed. Repeated violations will result in temporary or permanent suspension of community privileges.

4. Disclaimer

Posts in the community represent the personal experiences and opinions of individual users. They do NOT represent the views of SLTR Digital LLC, and they should NOT be treated as medical advice, nutritional prescriptions, or professional guidance. Always consult your healthcare provider before making health decisions based on information shared in the community. See our Medical Disclaimer for more.
