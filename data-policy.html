Data Policy.

Last updated: August 8, 2026 · Effective: August 8, 2026

This Data Policy sits alongside our Privacy Policy and describes, category by category, where your data lives, how it is encrypted, and how long we keep it. Where the two documents overlap, the Privacy Policy governs.

1. Data Processing Addendum

1.1 Health Data Classification

Nourished by Mira processes the following categories of health-related data. Categories marked device-only never reach our infrastructure. Categories that do reach our infrastructure are always encrypted in transit; encryption at rest is applied per category as listed below.

Medication brand name and exact dose (as you record it)

  • Sensitivity Level: HIGH
  • Never transmitted. This stays on your device.
  • Encryption: Encrypted at rest on device by iOS
  • Retention: Until you change or delete it, or delete the app

Anonymized medication class and dose tier

  • Sensitivity Level: HIGH
  • Transmitted only if Backup is enabled, as part of your profile, and to the AI service as described in the Privacy Policy
  • Encryption: Encrypted at rest with a customer-managed key, encrypted in transit
  • Retention: Duration of account + 30 days

Symptom logs

  • Sensitivity Level: HIGH
  • Transmitted only if Backup is enabled
  • Encryption: Encrypted at rest with a customer-managed key, encrypted in transit
  • Retention: Duration of account + 30 days

Body weight and composition

  • Sensitivity Level: MEDIUM
  • Encryption: Encrypted at rest on device by iOS
  • Retention: Device-only; body composition records are never transmitted. Current weight and goal weight are part of your profile and are transmitted only if Backup is enabled.

Nutrition logs (meals, macros, hydration)

  • Sensitivity Level: MEDIUM
  • Transmitted only if Backup is enabled
  • Encryption: Encrypted in transit. Stored without an additional layer of encryption at rest.
  • Retention: Duration of account + 30 days

Pantry items

  • Sensitivity Level: LOW
  • Transmitted only if Backup is enabled
  • Encryption: Encrypted in transit. Stored without an additional layer of encryption at rest.
  • Retention: Duration of account + 30 days

Dietary preferences and nutritional targets

  • Sensitivity Level: LOW
  • Transmitted only if Backup is enabled, as part of your profile
  • Encryption: Encrypted at rest with a customer-managed key, encrypted in transit
  • Retention: Duration of account + 30 days

Barcode scan history

  • Sensitivity Level: LOW
  • Encryption: Encrypted at rest on device by iOS
  • Retention: 30 days, device-only

1.2 Sub-processors

We rely on the following categories of third-party services. Where a provider is identified by name in our Privacy Policy, it is named here as well.

Backend infrastructure and authentication provider

  • Purpose: Hosting, encrypted storage, authentication (Amazon Cognito), and secure API endpoints
  • Data Accessed: Server-side backup data (encrypted), account email for sign-in

AI model service

  • Purpose: Powering Mira's nutritional guidance and meal generation
  • Data Accessed: the context listed in section 1.6 of the Privacy Policy, your typed messages, and any free text you wrote in the goal and movement fields. Never your name, email, medication brand, or exact dose.

Nutrition database provider (Open Food Facts)

  • Purpose: Food name and barcode lookups
  • Data Accessed: Food names and barcodes only (no user identity)

Apple (App Store and HealthKit)

  • Purpose: Subscription payment processing; health data integration
  • Data Accessed: Payment details are handled entirely by Apple and never reach us. Apple HealthKit data stays on-device. Reads: weight, lean body mass, body fat percentage. Writes: dietary energy consumed, dietary protein.

1.3 Apple App Store Requirements

Per Apple's Health & Fitness app requirements:

  • Health and fitness data collected through the App is never used for advertising
  • Health and fitness data is never sold to data brokers
  • Apple HealthKit data is never stored in any cross-device sync service
  • We do not write false or inaccurate data to Apple HealthKit
  • Users can revoke Apple HealthKit access at any time through iOS Settings

Per Apple's Spring 2026 Medical Device Disclosure requirement:

  • Nourished by Mira is declared as NOT a regulated medical device in any jurisdiction
  • The App does not function as a Software as a Medical Device (SaMD)
  • The App does not perform clinical diagnostic, monitoring, or therapeutic functions
