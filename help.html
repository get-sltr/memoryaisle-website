Help Center

Everything you need to know about MemoryAisle

Welcome to the MemoryAisle Help Center. MemoryAisle is an AI nutrition coach built for people on GLP-1 medications: it protects lean mass with a protein-first plan that adapts to your appetite, symptoms, and dose schedule.

Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Download MemoryAisle from the App Store, open the app, and sign up with a valid email address or a supported sign-in method. Your data syncs to your account automatically.

How is my plan built?

MemoryAisle calculates your daily protein, calorie, fat, and hydration targets directly from your current weight, goal weight, and medication schedule, grounded in muscle-preservation principles for rapid weight loss. You can always see why each target is what it is.

Are the targets medical advice?

No. Every target the app calculates is an informational estimate, not medical guidance. If you take a GLP-1 or any prescription medication, or have a medical condition, talk to your prescribing clinician or a licensed dietitian before acting on any target or meal plan.

What is Mira?

Mira is your AI nutrition coach. Tell her what you're craving, your goals, or your cuisine, and she generates recipes personalized to your macros. You can talk to her by voice or text, and she adapts to your protein deficit, training schedule, and dose cycle. Mira is not a medical professional, licensed dietitian, or healthcare provider.

Subscription & Billing

What is MemoryAisle Pro?

MemoryAisle Pro is the subscription that unlocks the full experience, including unlimited Mira coaching, personalized daily targets, meal planning, smart grocery lists, and receipt scanning.

How much does Pro cost?

  • Monthly: $9.99/month
  • Yearly: $49.99/year (about $4.17/month)

New subscribers start with a 2-week free trial. The subscription auto-renews and can be cancelled anytime through your Apple ID settings.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > MemoryAisle > Cancel. Your Pro features remain active until the end of the billing period, and cancelling never deletes your data.

Can I get a refund?

Billing is handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support. We cannot process refunds directly.

Account & Privacy

How do I change my email address?

Currently, email changes require contacting support. Email us at support@memoryaisle.app from your registered email address.

How do I delete my account?

Visit the Delete Account page or go to Settings > Account > Delete Account in the app. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Is my health data secure?

Yes. MemoryAisle is built with a zero-knowledge security architecture: your health data is encrypted at every layer, and your private journal never leaves your device. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

Does Mira store my conversations?

Mira conversations are processed by AI services to generate responses. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is shared with AI providers and what never leaves your device.

Troubleshooting

The app won't sync

  • Check your internet connection
  • Pull down on the main screen to force refresh
  • Sign out and sign back in

Mira isn't responding

  • Check your internet connection
  • Try closing and reopening the app

Voice input isn't working

  • Ensure microphone permissions are enabled in Settings > MemoryAisle
  • Speak clearly after tapping the microphone icon

My day count looks off

You can adjust your day count anytime in the app to match your actual journey; it stays consistent everywhere once set.
